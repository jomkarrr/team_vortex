# Demo Script — 4 minutes, 3 personas

Setup: app open on Home. Backend pill shows "Demo mode" — say it out loud:
"Ye app bina internet ke bhi chalta hai, kyunki hamare user ke paas hamesha net nahi hota."

1. **Ramulamma (Eligibility)** — Pension Dhundo kholo, persona ki jaankari pehle se bhari milegi
   (ya live bharo: 67, AP, rice card ✓, widow ✓). Result: NTR Bharosa Widow ₹4,000
   "banti hai", IGNOAPS bhi list me reason ke saath. Ek document untick hai —
   death certificate. Phir Status page pe wahi case: Verified, atka hai usi ek document pe.
   Line: *"System jhooti umeed nahi deta — jo bacha hai, wahi bolta hai."*

2. **Srinivas Rao (Status → Fix)** — Status me dusra case: **Failed**, wajah saaf likhi hai —
   Life Certificate jama nahi hua. "Fix dekho" dabao → Band Kyun Hua? pehla flow khulta hai:
   face authentication ke steps, fingerprint wali galti ki warning ke saath.
   Line: *"Bank sirf 'paisa nahi aaya' bolta hai. Hum wajah aur ilaaj, dono dete hain."*

3. **Lakshmi (Family Pension)** — Teesra case + troubleshooter ka family-pension flow:
   50% family pension, Form 10D checklist, aur excess-pension recovery wali warning.
   Line: *"Sabse mushkil din me parivaar ko daftar nahi, raasta chahiye. Hum raasta dete hain."*

Band karne ki line: "PensionSaarthi pension deta nahi hai — pension tak ka raasta
sabke liye saaf karta hai. Bina OTP maange, bina jhooti umeed diye."
